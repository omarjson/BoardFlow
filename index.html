About BoardFlow

BoardFlow was born from a simple belief: tools for thinking should be free and accessible to everyone.

The Problem

Most whiteboard and board apps lock their best features behind expensive subscriptions. Students can't afford $10-30/month. Small teams get nickel-and-dimed for every collaboration feature. Individual creators pay more for tools than they earn from them.

Our Solution

BoardFlow gives you everything — infinite canvas, sticky notes, rich text, sketches, file uploads, AI assistant, real-time collaboration, 35+ templates, dark mode, 15 languages — all for free. No trials. No "freemium" bait-and-switch. Just free.

How We Do It

We leverage modern infrastructure that scales efficiently:

  • Supabase handles auth and the database with a generous free tier
  • Puter.js provides AI and file storage on a "user pays" model
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts the app with unlimited bandwidth
  • Vanilla JS means no framework overhead — the app loads fast on any device

No backend server to maintain. No expensive infrastructure. Just a clean, fast web app that works.

Open Source

BoardFlow is MIT licensed and open source. You can inspect every line of code, self-host it, or contribute to the project on GitHub.

Our Vision

We envision a world where the tools you use to think, plan, and create don't depend on your ability to pay. Where a student in any country has the same access to a powerful whiteboard as a funded startup. Where "free" means free — not "free until we change our mind."

BoardFlow is our contribution to that world.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Introduction

BoardFlow ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google profile information (name, email, profile picture).

Board Content

All content you create on your boards — notes, sketches, files, images, audio, video — is stored in our database and associated with your account. We do not access or use your content for any purpose other than providing the service to you.

Usage Data

We collect minimal usage data (page views, feature usage) to improve the application. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the BoardFlow service
  • To authenticate your account and secure access
  • To store and sync your board content across devices
  • To communicate with you about your account (if needed)
  • To improve the application based on aggregated usage patterns

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, which uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. We do not operate our own servers — your data is managed by Supabase's secure PostgreSQL hosting.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Authentication and database hosting
  • Puter.js — AI features and file storage (user-pays model)
  • ImgBB — Image hosting for uploaded images
  • Cloudflare — Static site hosting and CDN

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

Data Retention

We retain your account and board data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Your Rights

  • Access all data associated with your account
  • Export your board data at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

Children's Privacy

BoardFlow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
